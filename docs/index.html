THE IDEA

Waze supplies the guidance.
Your car draws the card.

01 / GUIDANCE

The details that matter

Maneuver arrow, distance to the turn, next road, remaining route distance and time. Sent to the stock BYD guidance adapter.

02 / BUILT IN

One modified APK

The integration runs inside Waze. You keep the native navigation interface, without a separate companion application.

03 / LIFECYCLE

Updates, then clears

Guidance refreshes while the source is active. Ending the route clears the card. Stale source data expires instead of leaving an old instruction.

Experimental cluster map support is also included. It uses the separate Small / Full screen navi modes; physical map rendering still needs validation.

Changing maneuvers, distance countdown, complete field rendering, long sessions and other firmware remain unverified. Logged parked frames carried an unknown maneuver and zero turn distance.

The experimental build also recorded Waze route-dialog theme errors. Google sign-in is unverified.

A FEW MORE DETAILS

Good to know.

Will it work on every BYD?

No universal compatibility claim is made. Guidance targets the stock DiLink 5 adapter and has been tested while parked on our Tang EV with Android 12. An Android 10+ minimum does not mean every DiLink version has a compatible guidance adapter.

What about the windshield HUD?

On the reference car, the stock HUD follows the cluster guidance path. The owner confirmed that route cancellation cleared both. Detailed HUD field rendering and behavior on other vehicles still need validation.

Does it show lanes or arrival-time text?

No. This integration sends maneuver and road information, distances and remaining time. It does not send lane guidance or an arrival-time text field.

What happens if Waze is killed?

An abrupt process kill cannot send an immediate end message, so a card may remain until the next app start. The build saves an ownership flag and clears that stale card on restart.

Is this an official Waze or BYD release?

No. This is an independent experimental community project, with no affiliation to Waze, Google or BYD. The source and build instructions are available on GitHub.
